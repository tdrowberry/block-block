Free Tips

Complete three different campaign levels to earn one Tip. Earn another at six, nine, twelve, and every three completions after that, for up to 33 Tips across 100 levels. Previously completed levels count. Replays and tutorial lessons do not earn additional Tips.

During a puzzle, tap Tip to see your balance and progress toward your next Tip. Use one to preview up to 20 moves from your current position. A different move, restarting, or leaving the level clears that preview. If no route exists, restart the puzzle; no Tip is spent on an impossible route. Every level is solvable without Tips.

Learning and saves

Choose Learn to play for an optional interactive tutorial, or skip to level 1. Progress and unused Tips stay on this device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes them; there is no account or cloud restore.
