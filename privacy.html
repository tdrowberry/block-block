Your game data

Level progress, best scores, and control preferences stay on your device. The Android app also stores Tip credits and purchase tokens locally to prevent duplicate rewards. There is no game account or cloud save. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes progress and unused consumable Tips; consumed purchases cannot be restored across devices.

Optional ads and purchases

You can play every level without buying Tips or watching ads. In the Android app, you may choose a rewarded ad to earn one Tip or purchase a pack of five Tips through Google Play. A Tip reveals at most 20 moves. Closing an ad before earning its reward does not grant a Tip. Google Play handles payment details; Lalao Lemur does not receive your card details.

The Android advertising SDK may collect or share device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. This collection depends on SDK configuration and your consent choices. See Google's privacy policy. Where applicable, the app requests consent before ads and provides Ad privacy choices in Settings.

Browser version

The browser game stores progress and settings locally. It does not include the native advertising or billing SDKs. Your hosting provider may process ordinary server access logs.
