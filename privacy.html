Block Block privacy policy

A game by Lalao Lemur · Updated October 13, 2026 · Applies to version 1.2.0 and later.

A free, offline game

Block Block is free to play, with no advertisements, in-app purchases, accounts, analytics or tracking SDKs. The Android game does not collect or transmit personal information or game activity to Lalao Lemur. It does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or advertising-ID permissions.

Data on your device

Level progress, best scores, tutorial progress, sound and control preferences, and free Tips used are saved locally. You earn one Tip for every three different campaign levels completed. Existing completed levels count; replaying a level does not earn another Tip. These records are not uploaded or synchronized between devices. The game does not ask for your name, email, date of birth or age range.

Children and families

Children and older players can play the same game without ads or purchases. Children should ask a parent or guardian before contacting support. Please do not send personal information about children.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Browser players can clear this site's storage in their browser. Doing so removes progress and unused Tips. Android cloud backup of game data is disabled. No game account exists to delete. Older test versions may have saved age-range or test-wallet records locally; clearing app storage removes those records too.

Support and external pages

If you choose to email lalao.lemur@gmail.com, your email provider and ours process the message. We receive your email address and whatever information you include, use it to respond, and retain it only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence at the same address.

Opening a support link or this public website uses your browser or email app. The website is hosted by Render, which may process technical connection information such as IP addresses and request logs under its privacy policy. Google Play separately handles app distribution under Google's privacy policy. These external services are separate from the offline game.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the game's practices change. Questions or deletion requests: lalao.lemur@gmail.com.
