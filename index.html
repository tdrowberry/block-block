How to play

01 · MOVE

A swipe. A tip. A tumble.

Swipe up, down, left, or right anywhere on the board. You can also use the arrow buttons, keyboard arrow keys, or WASD. A swipe makes one move.

02 · ESCAPE

Find the perfect fit.

Land upright on the glowing exit at normal size: two squares tall. Complete the level to unlock the next. Cleared levels can be replayed.

03 · STAY ON THE TILES

Every edge counts.

The whole block must have support. Fall off and the level automatically starts over, resetting moves, size, and bridges. There is no undo. Use ↻ or R to restart, and LVL or Escape to return to levels.

LEVEL 11 · SIZE PADS

↑3 ↓1 =2

Touch a size symbol with any part of a safely landed block. It stands you upright on that pad: three squares tall, one cube, or the normal two. Leave and return to activate it again.

LEVEL 21 · BRIDGES

⏻ Make a way across.

Touch a switch to open or close all dotted bridges. Open bridges support every size. Closing one underneath you causes a fall.

LEVEL 31 · TELEPORTS

◎A A step through space.

Stand upright at size two or three to teleport to the matching letter. Cubes and lying blocks stay put. Your size and bridge state travel with you. Leave the destination before returning through it.
