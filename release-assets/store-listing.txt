TITLE
Block Block

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Help Block find his triangle son through 100 clever puzzles about belonging.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Different shapes. One family.

Join Block on a gentle journey to find his triangle son, who has set off because he feels he does not belong. Follow their story through 20 worlds, help them find each other, and bring them home together. A warm adventure about loving your family exactly as they are.

Guide your block across floating platforms and stand upright on the exit at normal size: two squares tall. Explore 100 puzzles with size-changing pads, bridges, portals and fragile tiles.

LEARN BY PLAYING
Try the optional interactive tutorial with guided swipes and practice moves, or skip straight to level 1. Replay it whenever you want.

MAKE EVERY MOVE COUNT
Complete a level to unlock the next. Replay favorites, improve your best score and match the minimum move count to earn a perfect star. Choose swipe controls or on-screen arrows, with optional sound and reduced-motion support.

A LITTLE NUDGE
Earn one free Tip for every three different campaign levels completed. Each Tip previews up to 20 moves from your current position. Replays and tutorial lessons do not earn extra Tips. Every puzzle can be solved without Tips.

COMPLETELY FREE
No ads. No purchases. No account needed. Play offline. Progress and unused Tips stay on this device and are lost if you uninstall or clear app data.
