Block Block privacy policy

A game by Lalao Lemur · Updated October 13, 2026

Pre-release notice: the public support contact and hosted policy must be configured before publication.

Your game data

Level progress, best scores, sound and control preferences stay on your device. The Android app also stores your chosen age range, Tip credits, newly completed level counts, and processed purchase tokens locally. Tokens prevent duplicate credits. There is no game account, cloud save, or developer-operated server receiving your game saves.

Children and families

Block Block is designed for children and older players. The Android app asks for an age range, with no answer selected in advance. That range stays on this device. It does not ask for your date of birth, name or email address to play. Every player receives the same child-directed advertising protections; choosing an older range does not unlock personalized ads. Parents or guardians should handle support and purchase questions and supervise any optional purchases. Google Play's parental controls and purchase approvals can be managed in the family's Google account.

Advertising and consent

The Android app uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). All ad requests are tagged for child treatment, with a maximum general-audience ad rating. Personalized and remarketing ads are disabled. The app removes advertising-ID and Android ad-services permissions. Google's SDK may still process IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), app interactions, diagnostic information and permitted identifiers such as app set ID to serve contextual ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. Child-directed treatment does not mean that no data is processed. Google handles this data under its policies and applicable child-protection requirements.

UMP is configured for users under the age of consent for every age range, so a personalized-ad consent form is normally not requested. Open Settings → Ad privacy choices to see any available controls or an explanation of these protections. The game does not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions. Read Google's privacy policy and Google's ad privacy controls.

Every level is playable without buying Tips. An automatic full-screen ad may appear after five newly completed levels, before the results buttons appear; replays do not count. Ads are skipped when unavailable and do not interrupt an active puzzle. A voluntary rewarded ad grants one Tip only when its reward is earned.

Optional purchases

Google Play processes payments for consumable packs of 1, 5, 10, 20, 30, 40 or 50 Tips and handles payment and transaction information under Google's policies. Lalao Lemur does not receive your card details. The app reads purchase status, product identifiers, quantity and purchase tokens to verify purchases, grant credits and prevent duplicate grants. Pending or canceled payments do not grant Tips.

Tips reveal up to 20 moves. Purchased and earned Tips are device-local, cannot be transferred, and are lost if you uninstall or clear app data. Consumed purchases cannot be restored on another device. See Help and purchase support for payment problems and Google Play refund requests. Your applicable consumer rights are not limited by this notice.

Security, retention and deletion

The Android app stores its game and wallet data in app-private storage and disables Android backup of that data. Google's Mobile Ads SDK encrypts transmitted data using TLS. These safeguards do not guarantee absolute security.

Local progress, age range, preferences, credits and processed purchase tokens are retained until you clear app storage or uninstall. To delete them, use Android Settings → Apps → Block Block → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This permanently removes unused Tips and progress. In the browser, clear this site's data in your browser settings. There is no game account to delete.

Clearing this app's data does not delete records held by Google, including transaction, advertising or consent records. Google determines retention of records it holds under its policies and legal obligations. Use Google's privacy controls or contact Google for requests concerning those records; contact Lalao Lemur for questions about the game. If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to provide are used to respond and resolve your request. Do not send payment card details, passwords or signing keys. Support correspondence is kept while resolving your inquiry and as needed for legal obligations or disputes; you may request deletion through the contact below, subject to those obligations.

Browser version

The browser version stores progress and settings on your device and caches game assets for offline use. It does not include the Android advertising or billing SDKs. A public website's hosting provider may process ordinary access logs, such as IP address and requested page, under its own policies.

The public privacy and support pages are prepared for Render hosting. The hosting provider may process technical request information when you visit those pages. See Render's privacy policy. The policy pages include no advertising scripts, analytics scripts or contact forms.
