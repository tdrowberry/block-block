Children: please ask a parent or guardian to contact support or help with purchases. Parents can use Google Play purchase approvals and parental controls to manage optional purchases.

For a game problem, describe what happened and include the level number, app version and device model if available. For a purchase problem, include your Google Play order ID and what went wrong. Do not send passwords, payment card details or signing keys.

Tips and purchases

All 100 puzzles can be played without Tips. One completed rewarded ad earns one Tip; the Tip store offers one-time Google Play packs of 1, 5, 10, 20, 30, 40 or 50. The price shown by Google Play applies. Any displayed pack savings compare its price with buying the same number of single Tips in the same currency. A Tip previews up to 20 moves from your current position. Deviating, restarting or leaving a level clears the preview.

Payment that is pending or canceled does not grant Tips. If a completed purchase has not appeared, reopen the app with an internet connection using the same Google Play account. The app checks outstanding purchases again. If the problem continues, contact support.

Unused Tips and game progress are stored only on this device. Uninstalling or clearing storage removes them. Consumed Tip purchases cannot be restored after reinstalling or on another device. Do not clear storage as a first troubleshooting step for missing credits.

Refund requests

You can request a refund through Google Play under its refund rules, or contact Lalao Lemur for purchase help. Eligibility depends on your circumstances and applicable law. This page does not limit your statutory consumer rights.

Privacy and deleting local data

Open Settings → Ad privacy choices for available consent controls. To delete local saves and credits, clear Block Block's storage in Android settings or uninstall. This cannot be undone. Read the privacy policy for details about data handled by the game and Google.
