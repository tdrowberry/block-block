TITLE
Block Block

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Roll, resize and teleport through 100 clever block puzzles.

FULL DESCRIPTION
A little balance. A little nerve. One perfect move at a time.

Tip your block across floating platforms and stand upright on the glowing exit. It sounds simple—until the shortest path takes you in the wrong direction.

100 PUZZLES, TEN CHAPTERS
Find your footing, change your shape, build bridges, step through portals, and learn which fragile tiles can take your weight. Later chapters combine these mechanics into surprising escapes. The final 30 master puzzles demand at least 50 moves, rising to a 108-move finale.

MAKE EVERY MOVE COUNT
Beat a level to unlock the next. Replay favorites, improve your personal best, and match the minimum move count to earn a permanent perfect star.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Choose swipe controls or on-screen arrows. Enjoy optional sound and reduced-motion support. The puzzles work offline, and your progress stays on your device.

A LITTLE NUDGE
Optional Tips reveal a trail of up to 20 moves from your current position. Earn one Tip by completing a rewarded ad, or choose a Tip pack from 1 to 50 through Google Play. Every level can be solved without Tips.

Contains ads and optional in-app purchases. An automatic ad may appear after five newly completed levels, between puzzles. Ads and purchases require an internet connection. Progress and unused Tips are device-local and are lost if app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Android ads use child-directed treatment for every player, with personalized advertising disabled. An age-range choice is stored on the device. Parents and guardians should supervise optional purchases.
